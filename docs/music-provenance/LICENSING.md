# Demo music licence evidence

Checked 20 September 2026. The source catalogue at https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/pieces.json supplied the selected track titles, ISRC identifiers and download filenames recorded in 2026-09-20.json.

The track renderer at https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html displays this notice for catalogue entries: “Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License”. It builds track pages by matching the ISRC to pieces.json. Each selected track has its own ISRC link in the provenance record.

The publisher’s licensing page, https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/, offers a free Creative Commons option requiring attribution. The licence is https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. This permits reuse and adaptation, including commercial use, with attribution, a licence link and disclosure of changes.

The prototype identifies Kevin MacLeod as the actual musician, retains original track titles, links sources and the licence, discloses re-encoding and loudness normalization, and provides downloadable attribution. The fictional artists and sleeves do not imply musician endorsement or artist partnerships.
